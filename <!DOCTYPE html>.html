Tragic Night in The Big Gala

Last night, the serene streets of Greenfield were disturbed by a heinous crime. Residents awoke to news of a tragic murder near the event of Town Gala on Hill Street. The victim found in the lake which is near the Gala building, identified as the famous businessman Mr. Jonathan Price. He was found with a flower on his body, lying on lakeside lifelessly. Police are urging witnesses to come forward.

Witness Reports

One resident reported hearing a loud noise around midnight. Another claimed to have seen a shadowy figure leaving the premises. The police are piecing together the clues, but they need your help to solve this case.

The Moon Lily, Essence Allure: At the center of the controversy.

Perfume Under Fire

A popular perfume brand, "Essence Allure," is facing serious allegations from its users. Multiple reports have surfaced claiming that the perfume contains a toxic flower, known as The Moon Lily, causing severe health issues. One user stated, "I experienced dizziness and nausea after using it." The accusations have led to public outcry and calls for an investigation.

Meanwhile, the owner of "Essence Allure," Ms. Clara Davenport, has strongly denied the claims. In a statement, she said, "Our products go through rigorous testing and meet all safety standards. These accusations are baseless and aimed at tarnishing our reputation."
